# Prospective protocol for binary approval-total questions

Question. In exact proportional approval voting (PAV), can one truthful answer to “Do you approve at most t proposals?” reduce the number of answers needed to certify the outcome, compared with “Do you approve proposal c?” Both responses convey a single yes/no bit. A count-threshold question may involve more effort. Compare equal response counts first and rerun at higher threshold-question prices.

## Rule and shared information

Twelve voters, five equally eligible proposals and two seats. Use the integer PAV scores and lexicographic tie order in `voting.py`. Each voter has one latent ballot, visible only to the evaluator. Both query interfaces start with the same empty transcript and may query any row. The expanded interface may ask either an unresolved approval bit or a threshold t in 0 through 4 on that row's approval count, with both answers feasible. The same exact worst-case margin certificate considers all feasible completions after every paid question. Output a committee only when its fixed-tie-order victory holds on all completions. Score the winner against full revelation. Threshold answers update an interval on the number approved. No numeric count is revealed for the price of one bit.

Use the posterior over each row obtained by conditioning the independent Bernoulli(.5) proposal model on that row's transcript. The query rule may use this working model; the certificate considers every feasible ballot. The leader maximises the resulting expected PAV score. Test two active query selectors under both interfaces. Each selector considers every permitted unresolved query and uses the same pre-seeded tie order over all voter-action pairs.

- Expected variance reduction: use up to three highest expected-score challengers. Score each query by its expected reduction in the variance of its ballot-level margin, weighted by one over one plus the expected score gap in ordinary PAV units. Evaluate both answers exactly.
- Certificate pressure: use up to three challengers with the greatest positive worst-case margin after tie correction. Weight by one over one plus the distance in ordinary PAV units from the greatest threat. Compute the expected reduction in each challenger's worst-case margin by evaluating both answers.
- Divide either score by the question's specified price. Fixed tie order is shared between interfaces. No selector is assumed to be globally optimal.

## Samples and endpoints

Use PCG64 and fresh seed bands. On the development set, use 48 clustered profiles starting at seed 421200. Select the better of the two approval-bit-only selectors by mean responses to certification. Keep the selected heuristic fixed in both interfaces for the primary comparison. Use 120 fresh clustered test profiles starting at 422200. Report both adaptive comparisons and which bit-only selector scored best after the fact. The primary endpoint is paired mean saving of mixed versus bit-only responses at unit prices, with a seeded 5,000-replicate 95% percentile interval resampling entire profiles. The declared practical gate is at least a five percent saving and a positive lower confidence endpoint. The decision certificate must make zero errors. Record both question types, actual PAV regret of the current expected-score leader at a cost of 30 approval responses, and certification coverage by 30 cost units. For weighted questions, take the checkpoint just before the first proposed response that would exceed 30, or at prior certification. Rerun the two matched primary policies at threshold prices 1.5 and 2. Approval questions cost one throughout.

Generate independent N(0,1) utilities in five columns. The clustered family has two latent factions of prior probabilities 0.60 and 0.40. Add 2.3 utility to the two faction-specific items and 0.70 to the shared fifth item. Draw lengths one, two and three with probabilities 0.26, 0.46, 0.28. Within each profile randomly relabel candidates and hold them fixed across methods. Secondary synthetic tests use 36 profiles each from fresh seed bands 423200 (independent utilities), 424200 (faction boost 3.6, shared boost 0.20, equal faction probabilities) and 425200 (2.3 boost to the same first two columns for every voter). Use the same length distribution in these three panels. No claim about the population of real elections follows from these generators.

External robustness panel uses the six sources and input checksums already fixed in `voting.py`. Retain only the previously eligible approval files. Take 20 independent projections per eligible source. Draw twelve ballots and five proposals uniformly without replacement within projection, with seeds 426200 + 100 times pinned source index + projection index. Project onto the chosen proposals and retain empty ballots. These are masked, counterfactual equal-size committee decisions using previously collected ballots. They do not measure real question effort or imply that PAV was the rule of those elections. File-level means give each eligible source equal weight. Do not treat projections from the same source as independent sources.

## Audit

Check the bounded-total formula against exhaustive row completions across all small feasible interval states. Independently verify complete-profile margins and certificates on tiny Cartesian products. Include the one-approval unanimous witness for the exact lower bound with only individual approval queries and the two-binary-requests-per-voter construction. Every paid reply must remove at least one feasible ballot, preserve the true one, and weakly tighten the certified margins. Record protocol hash and enough profile-level outcomes to replay the summary byte-for-byte.
